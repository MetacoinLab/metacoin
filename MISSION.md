# Mission

To build the credibly neutral capital base for humanity's expansion beyond Earth.

Gold was the money of the old world. Bitcoin became the money of the digital world. MetaCoin is designed to become the work-and-energy currency of the Space Machine Economy.

The protocol exists to fund and reward verifiable useful work toward the space economy — research, compute, energy, and robotics — performed by humans, AI agents, universities, and bounded-autonomous robots. It does this through two separated flows: a base currency mintable only by objective infrastructure work, and a fee-funded treasury that pays for judged mission work. No founders' cut, no investors, no hidden reserve.

This is a research-stage specification, not a product. No token exists. Not investment advice.
