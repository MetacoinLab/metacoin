# Roadmap

Proofs before token. No custom L1. Build credibility, standards, and demos first; launch a token only after the design and proofs are public and reviewed.

## Phase 0 — Constitutional repo (now)
Whitepaper, tokenomics, MIP-0001, MIP-0002, history, legal disclaimers. No token, no sale, no promises.

## Phase 1 — Agentic testnet (months 1–3)
Zero-value Test-META on the real ledger (not "points"). Ship the 30-day software agent demo: an agent earns Test-META for a verified task and spends it on compute.

## Phase 2 — Verification engine (months 3–6)
Build the three-gate stack from MIP-0002: vendor-agnostic TEE/attestation intake, the reproducibility pipeline with a computational-complexity ceiling, and the optimistic-oracle council process.

## Phase 3 — Treasury and first grants (months 6–9)
Small, real, milestone-based: an open-source lunar-structure simulation; a space-paper review agent; an orbital-servicing benchmark.

## Phase 4 — Token launch (legal-gated)
Only after counsel review and demonstrated traction. BTC and gold-backed pairs only.

## Phase 5 — MetaSpace research index (research only)
Public tracking framework. No product without licensed partners.

This roadmap is provisional and subject to change. Research only; not investment advice.
